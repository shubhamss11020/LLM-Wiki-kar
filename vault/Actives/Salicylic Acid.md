# Salicylic Acid

**Salicylic Acid** is an oil-soluble Beta Hydroxy Acid (BHA) that penetrates deep into sebaceous follicles to dissolve sebum plugs, exfoliate dead cellular debris, and prevent monsoon acne flare-ups on Indian skin.
