# Niacinamide

**Niacinamide** (Vitamin B3 / Nicotinamide) is a versatile, water-soluble active that fortifies the skin barrier, balances sebum production, and refines pore appearance.

## Mechanism of Action
- **Barrier Fortification**: Upregulates epidermal ceramide and free fatty acid synthesis, significantly reducing trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL).
- **Sebum Regulation**: Normalizes sebaceous gland excretion to control midday shine without compromising stratum corneum hydration.
- **Pore Refinement & Anti-Inflammatory**: Calms reactive redness, mitigates inflammatory signaling, and visually refines congested or enlarged pores.
- **Melanosome Transfer Inhibition**: Inhibits melanosome transfer from melanocytes to keratinocytes, fading post-acne marks (PIH).
