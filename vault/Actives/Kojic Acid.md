# Kojic Acid

**Kojic Acid** is a fungal-derived chelating active primarily used to address stubborn hyperpigmentation, melasma, and post-inflammatory marks on melanin-rich skin without inducing cytotoxic bleaching.

## Mechanism of Action
- **Copper Chelation at Tyrosinase**: Kojic acid chelates the copper ions present in tyrosinase's active site, arresting melanin synthesis.
- **Safe Melanin Modulation**: Targets focal hyperpigmentation without lightening baseline skin tone, establishing an even, radiant complexion.
