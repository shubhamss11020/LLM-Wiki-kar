# Vitamin C

**Vitamin C** (L-ascorbic acid and stable derivatives like Sodium Ascorbyl Phosphate) is a potent topical antioxidant and brightening active formulated for photoprotection, melanin regulation, and collagen synthesis.

## Mechanism of Action
- **Antioxidant Free Radical Scavenging**: Donates electrons to neutralize reactive oxygen species (ROS) triggered by UV radiation, particulate matter, and urban smog.
- **Tyrosinase Inhibition**: Modulates tyrosinase enzyme activity to reduce hyperpigmentation, post-acne blemishes, and sun spots.
- **Collagen Support**: Serves as an essential enzymatic cofactor for prolyl and lysyl hydroxylase in structural collagen synthesis.

## Application & Routine Guidelines
- **Time of Use**: Morning (AM) on cleansed skin before moisturizer and sunscreen.
- **Pairing Requirement**: Always layer under a broad-spectrum sunscreen ([[SPF & Sun Protection]]) to protect the active and boost photoprotection.
- **Results Timeline**: Noticeable surface radiance within 1–2 weeks; measurable tone equalization and dark mark fading within 4–6 weeks.
