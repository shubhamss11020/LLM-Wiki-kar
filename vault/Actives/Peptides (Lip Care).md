# Peptides (Lip Care)

**Peptides in Lip Care** (such as Palmitoyl Tripeptide-1 and Palmitoyl Tripeptide-38) stimulate fibroblasts to synthesize structural collagen and matrix glycosaminoglycans within the vermilion border for natural, non-irritating plumping.
