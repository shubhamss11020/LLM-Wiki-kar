Formulations that integrate clinically proven dermatological actives (Niacinamide, Peptides, Hyaluronic Acid, Squalane, SPF) directly into color cosmetics to deliver aesthetic payoff while simultaneously repairing the barrier.

## Clinical Synergy: Color + Active Treatment
By formulating color pigments in lipid carriers compatible with trans-epidermal penetration, hybrid cosmetics ensure that daily makeup wear does not degrade skin health, but actively prevents trans-epidermal water loss (TEWL) and hyperpigmentation.
