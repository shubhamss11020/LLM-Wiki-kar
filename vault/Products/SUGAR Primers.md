# SUGAR Primers & Base Preps

SUGAR's base prep collection designed to solve the two biggest foundation challenges in the Indian climate: excessive sebum breakthrough in the T-zone and uneven skin texture / enlarged pores.

## Product Formats

### 1. The Most Eligible Blurring Face Primer
- **Texture**: Velvety, translucent silicone-silica gel.
- **Function**: Fills and blurs open pores, fine lines, and uneven texture. Creates a smooth, matte canvas that grips foundation and prevents midday oxidation.
- **Best For**: Oily, combination skin, enlarged pores, and high-heat environments.

### 2. Aquaholic Hydrating Primer / Serum Drops
- **Texture**: Lightweight, refreshing water-burst gel.
- **Active Actives**: Hyaluronic Acid, Aloe Vera, and Green Tea extract.
- **Function**: Plumps dry or dehydrated skin, providing the ideal slip for dewy glass skin makeup without greasiness.
- **Best For**: Dry, dull skin, winter makeup prep, and AC environments.

### 3. Seal The Deal Eye Primer
- **Function**: Formulated with anti-crease film formers to absorb eyelid oils and lock kajal and eyeshadows for 16+ hours.

## Primer Selection by Skin Type & Goal

| Skin Concern | Recommended Primer | Texture | Primary Benefit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oily T-Zone / Open Pores | The Most Eligible Blurring Primer | Velvet Gel | 100% Matte, Poreless Canvas |
| Dry / Flaky / Dehydrated | Aquaholic Hydrating Primer | Water Essence | Plump, Dewy Glow Slip |
| Creasing Eyelids / Smudgy Kajal | Seal The Deal Eye Primer | Translucent Balm | 16HR Budge-Proof Eye Hold |

## Pro Application Rule
Apply a pea-sized amount onto the center of the face (T-zone and cheeks) using patting motions rather than rubbing to allow the silica microspheres to settle directly into pore hollows. Wait 60 seconds before applying foundation.
