## How to Find Your Shade

### The At-Home Undertone Tests

| Test | Warm Undertone | Cool Undertone | Neutral |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Vein test** | Greenish veins on wrist | Bluish/purple veins | Can't tell clearly |
| **Jewellery test** | Gold looks better | Silver looks better | Both look great |
| **Sun test** | Tan easily, rarely burn | Burn more easily | Mixed reaction |

### Reading SUGAR Shade Names
- **Warm names** (Latte, Macchiato, Cappuccino, Americano) → golden, honey undertones
- **Higher numbers** → deeper depth, not different undertone
- Match **undertone family first**, then fine-tune depth ±1 step

### Avoid Ashy or Orange
- **Ashy** = foundation too cool or too light for warm undertone → go one shade warmer/deeper
- **Orange** = shade oxidising or depth overshoot → need slightly cooler base; always judge after 20 minutes
