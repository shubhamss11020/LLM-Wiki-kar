## How to Apply for Maximum 18-Hour Hold

1. **Exfoliate** — use a lip scrub or damp washcloth to remove flakes
2. **Hydrate** — apply a thin lip balm 10 minutes before; blot excess before applying lipstick
3. **Line** — use a matching lip liner to create a barrier against bleeding
4. **Apply** — one thin, even coat from center outward; let it set 60 seconds
5. **Don't press lips together** — let each lip dry independently for a true transfer-proof seal
