## How to Apply for Maximum Hold

1. **Prep:** Blot any oil from lids, sweep a thin layer of translucent powder over the lid and lower lash line
2. **Tightline:** Look down into a mirror, gently lift the lid, press kajal into the roots of upper lashes and along the waterline in one smooth stroke
3. **Set:** Press a matte black or deep brown eyeshadow directly over the kajal with a small flat brush — this fuses with the waxy pencil and doubles staying power
4. **Lock:** Reapply a thin line on the lower waterline after setting the top; wait 30 seconds before blinking
