## What Makes It Different

Most matte lipsticks achieve long wear by using drying alcohols that strip moisture. The Matte As Hell formula uses **Dimethicone and Diisostearyl Malate** to create a smooth, flexible film instead of a rigid, drying one:
- True matte finish without cracking or flaking
- No settling into lip lines
- Retractable crayon tip gives sharp application without a separate lip liner
