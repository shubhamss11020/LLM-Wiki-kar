# All Set To Go Translucent Powder & Compacts

SUGAR's shine-eliminating powder arsenal, formulated with ultra-micronized silica and oil-absorbing minerals that lock foundation in place, blur enlarged pores, and provide 8+ hours of flash-free matte perfection in extreme Indian humidity.

## Product Formats

### 1. All Set To Go Translucent Loose Powder
- **Description**: Weightless, zero-pigment micro-milled loose powder.
- **Function**: Blurs pores, locks base without adding color or altering foundation shade. Zero flashback in photography.

### 2. All Set To Go Banana Powder
- **Description**: Warm yellow-tinted brightening setting powder.
- **Function**: Specifically tailored for Indian Fitzpatrick III–V skin tones. Brightens under-eye darkness and sets contour lines without ashy white casts.

### 3. Dream Cover SPF15 Mattifying Compact
- **Description**: Pressed powder compact enriched with Vitamin E and SPF 15 broad spectrum protection.
- **Function**: Quick touch-ups on the go, controlling midday sebum breakthrough.

## Comparison & Usage Guide

| Powder Type | Primary Function | Best Skin Tone / Area | Finish |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Translucent Powder** | All-over setting & blur | All skin tones (invisible) | Soft Velvet Matte |
| **Banana Powder** | Under-eye baking & brightening | Medium, Olive, Dusky skin | Radiant Bright Matte |
| **SPF15 Compact** | Midday touch-up & sun shield | Tone-matched (Fair to Deep) | Natural Matte |

## Pro Technique: The Humidity Bake
1. After concealer and foundation, press a generous layer of **Banana Powder** under eyes and around smile lines using a damp sponge.
2. Allow to sit ("bake") for 2–3 minutes while eye makeup is applied.
3. Dust off excess with a fluffy powder brush for a completely crease-free, sweat-resistant seal.
