# SUGAR Eyeliners & Graphic Liners

SUGAR's high-precision eye defining range, comprising ultra-matte felt tip pens, gel pencil pots, and jewel-toned metallic liners formulated to withstand Indian monsoons, high humidity, and oily lids.

## Product Formats

### 1. Eye Warned You So! Double Matte Eyeliner
- **Format**: Liquid dip-brush/felt pen with intense carbon black finish.
- **Wear**: 17+ hours waterproof and transfer-proof.
- **Best For**: Crisp cat eyes, dramatic wings, and humid summer days.

### 2. Born To Wing Gel Eyeliner
- **Format**: Gel pot with precision angled applicator.
- **Finish**: Rich, opaque matte film that sets in 30 seconds.
- **Best For**: Indian bridal makeup, tightlining, and long festivities where smudging is strictly prohibited.

### 3. Eye Told You So! Metallic & Colored Liners
- **Colors**: Emerald Green, Deep Navy, Bronze, Copper, and Rich Burgundy.
- **Best For**: Complementing deep brown eyes and warm Indian skin undertones during festive poojas and weddings.

## Pro Application Guide for Indian Eye Shapes

1. **Hooded / Oily Eyelids**: Apply thin lines close to the lash roots; avoid thick wings that fold into the crease. Set with translucent powder beforehand.
2. **Almond / Deep Set Eyes**: Extend the outer corner upwards toward the tail of the brow for an instant lift.
