## Why Lips Require Dedicated SPF
The skin on the lips is exceptionally thin (3–5 cellular layers vs 16 layers on the face) and contains virtually no melanin-producing melanocytes or sebaceous oil glands. Chronic UV exposure in Indian climates directly leads to:
1. **Hyperpigmentation & Darkening**: Uneven two-toned lips and dark lip perimeters.
2. **Loss of Collagen**: Accelerated thinning and fine vertical barcode lip lines.
3. **Severe Dehydration**: Flaking that ruins smooth matte lipstick application.

## Pro Pre-Makeup Protocol
Apply Tipsy Lips SPF 30 at the start of your skincare routine (15 minutes prior to makeup). Before swiping on your matte lipstick, gently blot the excess shine with a tissue so the matte film can adhere evenly.
