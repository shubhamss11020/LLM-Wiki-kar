# SUGAR POP Lipsticks

SUGAR POP is SUGAR Cosmetics' bold, pocket-friendly sister brand tailored specifically for Gen Z, college students, and first-time makeup experimenters in India. The lipstick lineup delivers high pigment, lightweight formulas, and trend-focused shades starting under ₹299.

| Feature | Detail |

## Key Benefits

- **Pocket-Friendly Pigment**: Intense color payoff at an entry-level price point without sacrificing safety or performance.
- **Formulated for Young Indian Skin**: Trendy berry, brown, terracotta, and pink hues curated to flatter Indian undertones.
- **Everyday Comfort**: Non-cakey, lightweight textures suitable for college lectures, internships, and casual hangouts.

## Top Shade Recommendations

- **Warm & Dusky Tones**: Deep Terracotta, Cinnamon, and Warm Cocoa nudes.
- **Fair to Medium Tones**: Peach Punch, Rosy Pink, and Coral pop.

- Beginners building their first makeup kit under ₹500 / ₹1000.
