# SUGAR Niacinamide Blush & Cheek Tints

The pioneering hybrid skincare-makeup flush that actively calms redness, balances sebum production, and strengthens the epidermal barrier with active **Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)** while delivering 12-hour sweat-proof cheek color.

## Product Formats

### 1. Contour De Force Face Blush (Velvet Powder)
- Micro-fine pigmented powder that diffuses softly over cheeks without emphasizing pores.
- Long-wear, humidity-tested, matte-to-satin finish.

### 2. Niacinamide-Infused Liquid/Cream Cheek Tint
- **Active Skincare Formula**: Formulated with 2% Niacinamide, Hyaluronic Acid, and Vitamin E.
- **Benefits**: Hydrates, controls midday sebum breakthrough on the apples of the cheeks, and blends effortlessly without disturbing the base foundation.

## Shade Matching for Indian Skin Tones

| Undertone | Best Blush Shades | Effect on Indian Complexions |
|---|---|---|
| **Warm / Golden** | Peachy Coral, Warm Terracotta, Apricot | Natural sun-kissed flush, no muddy cast |
| **Cool / Neutral** | Soft Mauve, Rose Pink, Dusty Berry | Fresh, youthful radiance |
| **Dusky / Deep** | Rich Plum, Spiced Rose, Burnished Berry | Intense, flattering warmth that never turns ashy |

## Pro Layering Technique for 12-Hour Endurance
1. **Base Tint**: Dab cream/liquid blush onto the apples of the cheeks and blend outward along cheekbones.
2. **Lock-In Powder**: Lightly dust a matching shade of powder blush directly on top.
3. This creates a sweat-proof sandwich that stays vibrant even through high humidity and long summer ceremonies.
