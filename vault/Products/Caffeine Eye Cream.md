## Biological Mechanism of Action

- **Vasoconstriction**: Caffeine penetrates the thin skin around the eyes to temporarily constrict dilated micro-capillaries that cause blue/purple vascular under-eye shadows.
- **Lymphatic Depuffing**: Stimulates fluid drainage, visibly reducing morning eye bags within 15–20 minutes.
- **Concealer Prep Canvas**: Smooths dehydration lines so under-eye concealers do not settle into creases or cake during baking.

## Pro Pre-Makeup Routine for Dark Circles
1. **Hydrate & Depuff**: Tap a rice-grain amount of Caffeine Eye Cream around the orbital bone with your ring finger. Wait 2 minutes.
2. **Color Correct**: Apply a peach/orange corrector to neutralize brown/grey pigment.
3. **Conceal & Set**: Apply tone-matched concealer and lightly press [[All Set To Go Translucent Powder|Banana Powder]] to lock.
