# Contour De Force Highlighters & Illuminators

SUGAR's luxury light-sculpting range, featuring baked and pressed highlighters crafted with ultra-refined micro-pearls that fuse seamlessly into Indian skin without accentuating texture, pores, or peach fuzz.

## Product Formats & Shades

### 1. Contour De Force Mini Highlighter
- **01 Champagne Champion**: A pale golden-champagne sheen curated for fair to light-medium skin tones.
- **02 Gold Glory**: A rich, luminous warm gold that melts effortlessly into classic Indian medium and olive complexions.
- **03 Royal Rose**: A rose-gold metallic glow perfect for neutral undertones and blush-highlighter hybrid looks.
- **04 Bronze Bliss / Honey**: A deep copper-bronze glow designed to impart a goddess sheen on deep dusky skin without silver chalkiness.

### 2. Own The Light Liquid Illuminator
- Lightweight liquid glow drops enriched with hydrators that can be mixed into moisturizers, foundations, or patted directly onto cheekbones and collarbones for festive candlelight radiance.

## Highlighters by Skin Tone & Undertone

| Skin Tone Range | Recommended Shade | Glow Undertone | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fair to Light** | Champagne Champion | Soft Ivory Gold | Subtle luminous lift |
| **Medium / Wheatish** | Gold Glory | Warm Honey Gold | Classic festive glow |
| **Olive / Tan** | Royal Rose / Warm Gold | Rose Gold / Amber | Sun-kissed radiance |
| **Deep Dusky** | Bronze Bliss | Copper Bronze | Rich, non-ashy molten glow |

## Pro Application Rule for Indian Skin
Avoid chunky glitter formulas. Apply across high points only (tops of cheekbones, bridge of the nose, cupid's bow, and brow bone) using a soft fan or tapered blending brush.
