# SUGAR Cosmetics

**SUGAR Cosmetics** is an Indian omnichannel beauty and cosmetics brand known for high-pigment, transfer-resistant, and climate-proof formulations designed specifically for Indian skin tones and humid weather.

## Core Brand Pillars
- **Formulated for Indian Skin & Climate**: Focus on yellow/golden undertone accuracy, high heat/humidity endurance, and long-wear pigments.
- **Cruelty-Free & 100% Vegan Commitment**: Certified cruelty-free with no animal testing across raw ingredients or finished products, and extensive 100% vegan product lines.
