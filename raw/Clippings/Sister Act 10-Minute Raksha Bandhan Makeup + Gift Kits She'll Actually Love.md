## How Do You Keep Rakhi Makeup Melt-Proof in August Heat?

### Set with mist

August in most of India means humidity that treats your base like a slip-n-slide. Set your look with a fine setting mist to fuse layers together — it stops powder from going patchy and keeps that dewy finish from sliding into oil-slick territory. If waterproof eyes are your worry, our [waterproof eye makeup for humid weather](https://www.sugarcosmetics.com/blogs/the-sugar-dairies/best-waterproof-eye-makeup-for-rainy-humid-weather) guide has you covered.

### Blot on the go

Carry blotting sheets or a mattifying compact for a mid-celebration touch-up. Press, don't rub — blotting lifts excess oil without disturbing your base, so you reset shine in seconds between the puja and the group photo. Your 18HR lip won't need reapplying, which is the whole flex.
