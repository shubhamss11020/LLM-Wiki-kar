## How Do You Use Your Beginner Makeup Kit in 5 Minutes?

This is your everyday face — fast, foolproof, and forgiving. Follow **The SUGAR Cosmetics Method**: prep, base, then colour-and-set. Each step builds on the last.

1. Prep your skin — Cleanse and moisturise. Hydrated skin grips makeup better and stops foundation from clinging to dry patches. In humid climates, let your moisturiser absorb fully before you start.
2. Build your base — Dot foundation across forehead, cheeks, nose and chin, then blend outward with a kabuki brush. Sheer is your friend — build coverage only where you need it.
3. Set with powder — Press banana powder into your T-zone and under-eyes to lock everything in place and brighten. This is the step that makes your look last past lunch.
4. Define your eyes — Line your upper lash line with waterproof kajal. Smudge for soft, leave sharp for crisp. Done.
5. Add colour — Swipe your lip twist across lips, then dab a touch onto the apples of your cheeks for an instant flush. Press, blend, go.

Dermatologists recommend a non-comedogenic, lightweight base for acne-prone and oily skin, which is common across Indian complexions in hot, humid weather. Research published in the *Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology* found that powder-based setting products significantly reduced surface shine and improved makeup wear time on combination skin — proof that your setting powder isn't an optional extra, it's the MVP.
