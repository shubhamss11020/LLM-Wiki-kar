## How Do You Apply Niacinamide Blush for a Lit-From-Within Flush?

The goal is a flush that looks like you just walked in from a brisk breeze — not two stripes of colour. Here's how to get a natural, sweat-surviving glow.

### Placement on the Cheeks

Smile, and find the apples of your cheeks — that's your anchor point. Apply your **blush for oily skin** there, then blend upward and outward towards your temple. This lifts the face and keeps colour away from your oiliest zones near the nose. For a more sculpted, awake look, drift a touch higher onto the cheekbone. Keep the concentration soft near the centre of the face where oil surfaces fastest.

### Layering Over Powder

This is the non-negotiable for oily skin: set your base first. Dust a thin layer of a translucent powder over your foundation before blush goes on. Our [All Set To Go Translucent Face Powder](https://www.sugarcosmetics.com/products/all-set-to-go-translucent-powder) creates a dry, even surface so your blush grips and blends smoothly instead of grabbing onto wet foundation and going patchy. Cosmetic scientists recommend this powder-then-blush sequence precisely because a mattified surface holds pigment far longer than a dewy, oily one.

### Building Slowly

The fastest way to look overdone is to go in heavy on the first pass. Start with a light hand, tap off excess, and build in thin layers until the flush looks right. A **cream blush in humidity** especially rewards patience — a little goes a long way, and thin layers meld into skin rather than sitting on top. You can always add more; taking it back is a whole ordeal.
