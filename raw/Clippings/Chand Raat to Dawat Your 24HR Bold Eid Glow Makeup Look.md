## How Do You Build a 24HR Soft Glam Base Step by Step?

Your base is the foundation of every festive look — literally. Here's how to make it last from Chand Raat to dawat without a single mid-feast meltdown.

### Step 1: Prime and moisturise

Start on hydrated skin. A pore-minimising primer smooths texture and gives foundation something to cling to. For oily-combination Indian skin in humid weather, prime through the T-zone to control shine before it starts. Research published in the *Journal of Cosmetic Dermatology* found that silicone-based primers significantly reduce sebum-related makeup breakdown over an 8-hour wear period — exactly what a long dawat demands.

### Step 2: Build a second-skin foundation

Swipe your foundation stick in thin layers and blend with a damp sponge for that seamless, second-skin finish. SUGAR's shade range spans warm-to-deep undertones, so Fitzpatrick III–V complexions find a true match instead of a grey or ashy cast. Build coverage only where you need it — leaving the high points slightly sheer keeps the look glowy, not flat.

### Step 3: Conceal and set

Brighten under the eyes and tap concealer over any redness around the nose. Then lock it all down with a dusting of setting powder through the T-zone. **Dermatologists recommend** setting powder over cream products to extend wear and prevent creasing — non-negotiable for a 12HR-plus festive face. If your skin runs oily, our [guide to translucent setting powder for oily skin](https://www.sugarcosmetics.com/blogs/the-sugar-dairies/translucent-setting-powder-oily-skin-india-baking-guide-2026) breaks down the baking technique that keeps shine at bay all evening.

### What is the best foundation finish for a glowing Eid makeup look?

A satin or dewy-natural finish foundation is best for an Eid glow, since it mimics healthy, lit-from-within skin without looking greasy.
