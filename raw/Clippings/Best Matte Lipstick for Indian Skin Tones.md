## How to Pick the Right Matte Shade for Your Skin Tone

| If you have... | Your undertone is likely... | Shades that work best |

## FAQs

### What is the best matte lipstick for Indian skin overall?

For the widest range of shades tested specifically on Indian skin tones, SUGAR's Matte As Hell Crayon Lipstick is a strong starting point. For budget-friendly daily wear, Lakme 9 to 5 remains one of the most dependable options.

### What are the best matte lipstick shades for Indian skin generally?

Warm undertones do well in terracotta, peach, and brown nudes. Cool undertones suit berry, plum, and rose pink. Neutral undertones can wear almost anything, with dusty rose and classic red being the most universally flattering choices among the best matte lipstick shades for Indian skin.

### Which matte lipstick for Indian skin tone lasts the longest?

Maybelline SuperStay Matte Ink and SUGAR's Matte Attack Transferproof Lipstick are both built specifically for long, transfer-resistant wear, holding through meals and coffee without frequent touch-ups.

### How do I find matte lipstick shades for dusky skin that do not look ashy?

Prioritize warm browns and deep berries over pale pinks and light nudes. Swatch on your actual lips rather than your hand, since lip pigmentation changes how a shade reads compared to skin.
