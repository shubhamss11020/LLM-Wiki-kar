## Cruelty-Free vs Vegan Makeup: What's the Difference?

Cruelty-free means a product is not tested on animals, while vegan means it contains no animal-derived ingredients — SUGAR Cosmetics is both. ### Can a product be vegan but not cruelty-free?

Yes, a product can be vegan but not cruelty-free, and this trips up a lot of shoppers. Vegan means the formula contains zero animal-derived ingredients, while cruelty-free means neither the product nor its ingredients were tested on animals. A brand could use fully plant-based ingredients yet still test on animals — or sell in markets that legally mandate animal testing — making it vegan but not cruelty-free.
