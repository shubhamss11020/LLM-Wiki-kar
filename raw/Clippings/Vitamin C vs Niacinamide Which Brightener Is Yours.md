## The Quick Verdict: Vitamin C vs Niacinamide, Sorted

Vitamin C is best for radiance and even tone while niacinamide balances oil and supports the barrier. You can use both together, applying vitamin C first in the morning and niacinamide after. They are compatible, non-competing actives, so pairing them gives you brightness, oil control and a stronger skin barrier without irritation for most skin types.

### What each does best

Vitamin C (ascorbic acid and its derivatives) is an antioxidant that neutralises free radicals and interrupts excess melanin production, which is why it's the go-to for dullness, uneven tone and post-blemish marks. Niacinamide, meanwhile, is the multitasker: it moderates sebum, tightens the look of pores and boosts ceramide production to keep your barrier resilient against pollution and humidity.

### Can you use both?

Yes — the old myth that they "cancel each other out" has been debunked by modern formulation science. They're stable together in well-made products. If you're new to actives, start one at a time, then layer once your skin is happy.

### Is niacinamide safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, niacinamide is one of the safest actives for sensitive skin because it strengthens the skin barrier and reduces redness rather than irritating it. Unlike some acids and high-strength vitamin C, niacinamide rarely causes stinging or sensitivity, which is why it suits reactive and acne-prone skin beautifully. Start with a 5-10% concentration and use it once daily, then build up as your skin adjusts. Always patch-test a new product on your inner arm for 24 hours before applying it to your face

### How long does it take to see results from vitamin C serum?

Most people see visible brightening from a vitamin C serum in about 4 to 6 weeks of consistent daily use. Early glow and hydration can appear within the first two weeks, but fading dark spots and evening out tone takes patience and regular application. For best results, apply vitamin C every morning on clean skin, follow with moisturiser and SPF, and store the serum away from direct light to keep it potent. Skipping days or inconsistent use will slow your progress significantly.

### What are the benefits of niacinamide for oily skin?

Niacinamide is excellent for oily skin because it regulates sebum production, visibly minimises enlarged pores, and reduces shine without stripping the skin. It also strengthens the skin barrier, which helps prevent the dehydration that often triggers oily skin to overproduce more oil. On top of that, niacinamide fades post-acne marks and calms inflammation, making it a smart choice for oily, breakout-prone complexions. Use it daily at 5-10% concentration, ideally under a lightweight moisturiser

### Can I use vitamin C with retinol?

It is best not to layer vitamin C and retinol together in the same routine, as both are potent actives that can cause irritation when combined. The smart approach is to use vitamin C in your morning routine for antioxidant protection and glow, and save retinol for your night routine to support cell turnover.
