## How Do You Apply Your Starter Face Step by Step?

This is **The SUGAR Cosmetics Method** for beginners: build in layers, blend as you go, and let each product do one clear job. Follow these numbered steps.

1. Prep and baseStart on clean, moisturised skin. Swipe foundation stick in short stripes across the centre of your face and blend outward with fingertips — warmth from your hands melts it into skin.
2. Set the T-zoneDust setting powder over your forehead, nose and chin where oil shows up first. Keep the cheeks lighter for a natural finish.
3. Add lip colourLine and fill with the matte crayon. Tap a tiny bit onto the apples of your cheeks and blend for an easy monochrome flush.
4. Top with shineDab tinted lip oil over the centre of your lips for dimension, or wear it alone on no-fuss days.

### 5-minute everyday look

For daily and office wear, keep the base sheer, set only the T-zone, and stick to a nude crayon finished with lip oil. Done in five minutes, this look survives commutes and canteen runs.

### Dressing it up

For festive nights and parties, layer the foundation for fuller coverage, set all over, swap to a deep berry crayon, and skip the oil for a crisp matte lip.
