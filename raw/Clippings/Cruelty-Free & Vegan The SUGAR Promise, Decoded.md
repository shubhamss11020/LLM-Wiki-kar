## What Makes Makeup Cruelty-Free?

Makeup is cruelty-free when no animal testing happens at any stage of production — not on the finished product, not on individual ingredients, and not through any third party or supplier. A truly cruelty-free brand also refuses to sell in markets where animal testing is legally required, keeping the no-testing promise unbroken from lab to lip.

### Cruelty-Free Defined

Here's the thing the marketing rarely spells out: "cruelty-free" is about the testing, not the ingredients. A brand earns the label by guaranteeing that neither it nor its suppliers test on animals at any point. That's why we lead every formula with this promise — our 12HR and 24HR longevity claims mean nothing if they come at an animal's expense. Cosmetic scientists note that modern safety testing relies on advanced in-vitro models and reconstructed human skin tissue, which predict irritation far more accurately than outdated animal tests ever did. For more on how these labels overlap, our breakdown of [what clean beauty actually means](https://www.sugarcosmetics.com/blogs/the-sugar-dairies/clean-beauty-brands-india-cruelty-free-vegan-guide-sugar) unpacks the jargon further.

### Vegan vs Cruelty-Free

These two get tangled constantly, so let's untangle them. **Cruelty-free** means no animal testing. **Vegan** means no animal-derived ingredients — no beeswax, no lanolin, no carmine, no animal-sourced collagen. A product can be one without the other, which is exactly why ingredient-aware shoppers learn to check for both. SUGAR formulas are vegan-friendly, so you get the testing-free and the ingredient-free promise in one swipe.
