## Cruelty-Free vs Vegan: What's the Difference You Need to Know?

People use cruelty-free and vegan interchangeably, but they answer two different questions. Cruelty-free asks: did this product test on animals? Vegan asks: does this product contain animals? A mascara can be cruelty-free and still pack carmine — which makes it not vegan. Knowing the gap protects your wallet from clever labels.

### What cruelty-free certification actually means

Cruelty-free certification confirms no animal testing occurred at the ingredient, formulation, or final-product stage.
