### Cruelty-free & vegan

Every SUGAR POP product is 100% cruelty-free and vegan — and crucially, carmine-free, meaning no crushed-insect pigment in your reds and pinks. Dermatologists recommend carmine-free formulas for sensitive eyes and lips because cochineal-derived carmine is a known contact allergen.
