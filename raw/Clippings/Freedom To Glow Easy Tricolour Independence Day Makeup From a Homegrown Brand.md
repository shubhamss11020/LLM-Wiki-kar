## How Do I Do a Tricolour Makeup Look You Can Actually Wear to Office?

To do a tricolour makeup look for Independence Day, sweep a soft saffron shade across your eyelids, dab a white or pearl shade into the inner corners to brighten, and add a thin green liner along the upper lash line. Keep skin fresh and natural, skip bold lips, and paint your nails in saffron, white and green for a subtle finishing touch.

### Subtle, Not Costume

The trick is dilution. You want the colours to be legible as saffron, white and green — but muted, blended and grown-up. Nobody needs a full block of neon orange on the lid at a Monday standup. Diffuse edges, sheer washes and a single accent are what keep this look chic rather than theme-party.

### What You Need

- A warm-toned **eyeshadow palette** with saffron, cream and green shades so you can control intensity in one place.
- A waterproof black kajal to anchor the lash line before you add colour.
- A dewy, buildable base that survives August humidity without sliding.
- Nail lacquer in the three tones for an easy, no-fuss tricolour moment.

## What Is the Step-by-Step for a Tricolour Eye?

The eye is where the whole look lives, so we're giving it the most detail. Build in thin layers — Indian summer heat and humidity are unforgiving, and a heavy hand tends to crease by noon. For Fitzpatrick III to V skin tones, warm saffron and deep bottle green are especially flattering because they echo the golden undertones most Indian complexions carry, making the eye look intentional rather than pasted-on.

### Saffron Wash

Using the saffron shade from the [Blend The Rules Eyeshadow Palette](https://www.sugarcosmetics.com/products/blend-the-rules-eyeshadow-palette), pack colour across the mobile lid and buff the edges into the crease. A fluffy dual-ended brush makes the blend seamless.

### White Brightener

Tap a cream or pearl shade into the inner corners and just under the brow bow. This is your "white" — it opens the eye and photographs beautifully at flag hoisting. **Cosmetic scientists note** that a light-reflecting shade placed at the inner corner exploits the way our eyes perceive contrast, creating the illusion of a wider, more awake gaze.

### Green Liner Accent

Anchor with a waterproof black kajal along the upper lash line, then trace a fine green line just above it or along the lower lash line. Waterproof formulas matter here — research on cosmetic wear in tropical conditions, including work published in the *International Journal of Cosmetic Science*, has shown that film-forming, water-resistant pigments hold significantly better against sweat and humidity than standard formulas. That's exactly the kind of monsoon-adjacent August weather you're dressing for.
