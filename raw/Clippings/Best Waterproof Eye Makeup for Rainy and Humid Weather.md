## 5 Monsoon Eye Makeup Tips That Actually Help

1\. Let each layer dry before adding the next. This is the single most important monsoon makeup habit. Kajal, eyeliner, mascara: let each product set for 20 to 30 seconds before you move on. Layering wet products on top of each other is why eye makeup smudges in humidity.

2\. Use concealer instead of eyeshadow primer. A thin layer of long-wear concealer on the lid, set with translucent powder, creates a dry, grippy base that holds eyeshadow and eyeliner in place better than most primers in humid conditions.

3\. Keep blotting papers in your bag. Oil buildup around your eyes is what makes waterproof products eventually break down. Blotting around (not on) your eyes once or twice during the day extends wear time significantly.

4\. Avoid cream eyeshadows during peak monsoon. Powder shadows set and stay. Cream shadows slide. Save the creamy formulas for winter and stick to pressed powder palettes from July through September.

5\. Carry your kajal for one midday touch-up. Even the best waterproof kajal benefits from a quick waterline reapplication after lunch. Five seconds, and your eye look stays sharp.

### Does waterproof eye makeup damage your eyes?

Not if the formula is ophthalmologist-tested. SUGAR kajals and eyeliners are dermatologically and ophthalmologically tested. Remove waterproof makeup gently at night with a dedicated eye makeup remover or micellar water.

### How do I remove waterproof eye makeup without rubbing?

Soak a cotton pad in micellar water or oil-based makeup remover, hold it against your closed eye for 10 seconds, then gently swipe downward. The soaking step dissolves the waterproof film so you do not need to rub or tug.
