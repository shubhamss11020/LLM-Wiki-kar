## Your Oily Skin Isn't the Problem — Your Makeup Routine Is

If you live in India and have oily skin, you've probably tried everything — blotting sheets by noon, three layers of powder, skipping moisturiser entirely. And your makeup still melts by 3 PM.

Here's the truth: oily skin doesn't need less makeup — it needs the **right makeup in the right order**. This guide covers every product category, the exact routine, and the products that genuinely survive Indian heat and humidity.

## Step-by-Step Makeup Routine for Oily Skin (Lasts 10+ Hours)

### Step 1: Prep with an Oil-Free Moisturiser

Yes, even oily skin needs moisturiser. Skipping it makes your skin produce MORE oil to compensate.

**What to use:** A lightweight, gel-based, oil-free moisturiser with niacinamide (controls sebum) and hyaluronic acid (hydrates without oiliness).

### Step 2: Apply a Mattifying Primer

This is the single most important step for oily skin. A good primer creates a barrier between your skin's oil and your makeup.

- Silicone-based primers fill pores and create a smooth base
- Look for "oil-control" or "pore-minimizing" on the label
- Avoid "dewy" or "illuminating" primers

**SUGAR Pick:** [SUGAR's Mattifying Primer](https://www.sugarcosmetics.com/collections/primer) controls shine for up to 8 hours while blurring pores.

### Step 3: Use an Oil-Free, Matte Foundation

- Always choose "matte" or "natural matte" finish
- Check for "oil-free" on the label
- Avoid "dewy", "radiant", or "luminous" foundations
- Use a damp beauty sponge (not fingers) for application

**SUGAR Pick:** [SUGAR Dream Cover Mattifying Compact](https://www.sugarcosmetics.com/collections/compact) delivers a flawless matte finish that controls oil through India's most humid days.

### Step 4: Set with Compact or Loose Powder

| Feature | Compact Powder | Loose Powder |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oil control | Good (4-6 hrs) | Better (6-8 hrs) |
| Portability | Excellent | Messy for travel |
| Best for | Touch-ups | Initial setting |

**Application hack:** Use the "baking" technique on your T-zone — apply generous powder, wait 5 minutes, then dust off excess.

## Top 5 Makeup Mistakes Oily-Skinned Indians Make

1. **Skipping moisturiser** — Dehydrated skin overproduces oil.
2. **Over-powdering** — Too much powder looks cakey and triggers more oil.
3. **Using fingers for foundation** — A beauty sponge gives better results.
4. **Choosing dewy everything** — Dewy + Indian humidity = greaseball.
5. **Not prepping the T-zone separately** — Your T-zone needs extra primer and powder.

## Monsoon-Proof Your Makeup

- **Double-prime** your T-zone during July-September
- **Switch to waterproof everything** — kajal, mascara, eyeliner
- **Carry blotting papers** — blot before powdering
- **Use setting spray twice** — once after foundation, once after full makeup

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What type of makeup is best for oily skin?

Oil-free, matte-finish products. Use a mattifying primer, oil-free foundation, and set with powder. Choose waterproof eye makeup and transfer-proof lipstick.

### Should oily skin use moisturiser before makeup?

Yes, always. Use a lightweight, oil-free, gel-based moisturiser. Skipping moisturiser causes excess oil production.

### How to stop makeup from melting in Indian summer?

Use a mattifying primer, set with loose powder using the baking technique on your T-zone, and finish with setting spray.

### Is compact or loose powder better for oily skin?

Loose powder provides superior oil control for initial setting, while compact powder is better for on-the-go touch-ups. Use both.
