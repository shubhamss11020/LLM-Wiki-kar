## FAQ

### What should a beginner buy first in makeup?

Start with three products: a BB cream for a natural base, a kajal for eye definition, and a lipstick or tint that suits your skin tone. Those three cover most of an everyday look.

### Is ₹500 enough for good quality makeup?

Yes. Every product on this list is under ₹500 and formulated for Indian skin and weather. Genuinely good makeup products under 500 exist across every category, from base to lips. The SUGAR POP BB Cream at ₹249 has over 1,000 reviews, and Lakme Eyeconic Kajal remains one of the most trusted names in the country at a similar price.

### Which brands make the best makeup kit under 500 in India?

SUGAR, Lakme, Maybelline, and Swiss Beauty are consistently the most recommended for beginners looking for makeup products under 500, each covering different strengths: SUGAR for base and lip formulas built for Indian skin, Lakme for reliable kajal and lip tints, Maybelline for budget eye makeup, and Swiss Beauty for affordable concealers and eyeshadow.

### What order should I apply makeup in as a beginner?

Sunscreen first, then BB cream or foundation, then compact powder to set it. After that, move to eyes (concealer, kajal, mascara in that order) and finish with lips.
