## What Does Niacinamide Do for Indian Skin Glow?

Niacinamide has quietly become the most-loved active in Indian skincare, and it deserves the hype. It works with your skin, not against it — supporting the moisture barrier, calming visible redness and helping your complexion look more even and radiant over time. Think of it as your everyday glow insurance.

**Niacinamide Spotlight:** INCI name Niacinamide (vitamin B3). It supports ceramide production in the skin barrier and helps regulate sebum, which visibly reduces excess shine and improves the look of texture and tone. The result reads as a softer, more even, glowier complexion.

### The ingredient, decoded

Niacinamide is water-loving, gentle and famously well-tolerated, which is why cosmetic scientists often call it the "does-it-all" active. It supports the production of ceramides — the lipids that keep your barrier strong and your skin bouncy — while helping to visibly refine the look of pores and uneven texture. Research published in the *International Journal of Cosmetic Science* found that topical niacinamide can improve the appearance of skin tone evenness and reduce visible sebum, which is exactly why it pairs so beautifully with makeup rather than fighting it.

### Why it suits Indian skin tones

For Fitzpatrick III–V skin — the medium to deep tones most common across India — glow can sometimes get lost under mattifying, cakey formulas. Niacinamide helps your skin look naturally luminous and even, so your undertones read rich and healthy rather than flat. Combine that with India's humid, sweat-heavy climate and you've got the perfect case for an active that balances oil while keeping that dewy finish alive. It's brightening in the true sense — enhancing radiance, never altering your beautiful natural depth.

**Key Takeaway:** Niacinamide is a barrier-supporting, oil-balancing form of vitamin B3 that makes Indian skin look more even and radiant — a glow story, not a fairness one. Used both in your serum and now in glow blush, it delivers a lit-from-within finish that survives Indian heat.
