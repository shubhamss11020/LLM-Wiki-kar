## Frequently Asked Questions

### What colour eyeliner makes brown eyes pop?

Navy blue and emerald green make brown eyes pop the most. Both sit opposite the warm tones in brown irises, so the contrast reads instantly and brightens the whites of your eyes more than a same-tone black or brown liner.

### Is black or brown eyeliner better for brown eyes?

Both work, for different reasons. Black frames the eye with contrast and suits evening looks. Brown blends into the iris for a softer finish that holds up through a full workday without looking harsh under office lighting.

### Which colour complements brown eyes the most?

Plum and burgundy work through harmony, since their red undertones echo the warmth already in a brown iris. Navy and emerald work through contrast instead, and which one suits you comes down to your skin's undertone more than your eye colour.

### Which eyeliner is best for dark brown eyes?

Metallic bronze or copper works best on dark brown eyes, since the shimmer catches light and brings out golden flecks that are harder to see in deeper irises. Prefer no shimmer? A matte navy liner gives you the same lift through contrast instead of sheen.

### What makes brown eyes pop more?

Colour choice matters, but placement matters just as much. A thin line along just the upper lash line, paired with a warm highlight in the inner corner, opens up brown eyes more than a heavy all-around line, even on a rushed morning with two minutes before you're out the door.
